# Meridian — Roadmap

A living checklist against the full "world's best" vision, tracked here
so it survives across updates rather than living only in one chat.
Updated alongside CHANGES.md as each piece is actually built AND
verified working — not marked done just because it was discussed.

**Legend**: ✅ built and verified · 🔶 partially built · ⬜ not started

---

## CHALLENGE — the live quiz engine (Meridian's core, and it's solid)

- ✅ Live, timed, presenter-controlled sessions
- ✅ Speed-bonus and standard scoring
- ✅ Real-time leaderboard
- ✅ 61-language live translation
- ✅ AI feedback tailored to the specific wrong answer picked
- ✅ Image and video questions
- ✅ Self-healing session engine (survives a dropped connection or a stuck browser)

## MASTER — assessment & retention

- ✅ Durable, permanent answer log (knowledge gaps survive past 24 hours)
- ✅ Self-vs-own-history radar chart
- 🔶 Persistent XP across every quiz someone takes — streaks and levels not built yet
- ⬜ Scheduled retention re-testing (7-day / 30-day follow-up)

## CERTIFY — genuinely a strength already

- ✅ Premium, purpose-designed certificate
- ✅ Unique certificate ID + real, working QR verification page
- ✅ Real handwritten signature upload
- ⬜ Multi-tier certification (Brand → Collection → Product → Technical → Sales → Mastery)

## COACH — AI that helps a trainer, not yet a learner directly

- ✅ Trainer Assistant — ask about real team data, grounded, never invents numbers
- ⬜ Proactive "recommended for your team" surfaced without being asked
- ⬜ AI coach speaking directly to an individual learner ("you understand X, work on Y")

## LEARN — microlearning content between questions

- 🔶 Info pages (content slides mid-quiz) — database and scoring-safety done; no editor UI yet, doesn't render live yet

## PRACTISE / APPLY — beyond standard multiple choice

- ⬜ Scenario questions ("a customer says X, how do you respond?") with graded response quality

## DISCOVER — a content library before quizzing

- ⬜ Doesn't exist — quizzes are standalone, not tied to browsable training material

## CONTENT CREATION

- ✅ Export a quiz to PowerPoint
- 🔶 PDF / PowerPoint / URL → AI-generated questions — all three extraction methods tested and confirmed working; not yet wired into a full generate-and-review flow
- ⬜ Generate from a bare topic with no source material — deliberately deferred as a real hallucination risk for niche, private-label brands

## GAMIFICATION

- ✅ XP
- ⬜ Levels
- ⬜ Streaks
- ⬜ Badges
- ⬜ Daily challenges / missions
- ⬜ Store-vs-store and regional team competitions

## WATCH-SPECIFIC INTERACTIONS

- ⬜ None built yet — rotate a bezel, tap a crown, explore a movement, etc.

## ENGAGEMENT FORMATS BEYOND QUIZZES

- ⬜ Polls
- ⬜ Word clouds
- ⬜ Q&A (audience-submitted, upvoted questions for a presenter)

## LEARNER EXPERIENCE

- ⬜ A real "My Meridian" home/profile screen
- ⬜ Learning paths

## ADMIN & ANALYTICS

- ✅ Multi-user accounts, per-user quiz ownership and limits
- ✅ Trainer Assistant natural-language analytics
- ⬜ Visual analytics dashboard (charts, not just Q&A)

## PLATFORM

- ✅ Standalone Meridian brand identity
- ✅ AI safety principle applied consistently — never invents facts about a specific product/brand without real source material
- 🔶 Multi-tenant SaaS architecture — discussed as the "Path B" direction, not built; still a single company's data model today
